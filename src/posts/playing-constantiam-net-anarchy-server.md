I started playing Minecraft again after a long break.\
This time, I wanted one simple thing: **a server where my builds stay safe for years**.

I first joined **budgie.network**, a semi-vanilla server that promised no resets.\
I built a few houses there and enjoyed the slow and calm style.

But then my old interest came back.\
I missed **anarchy servers** — the chaos, the danger, the feeling that every block matters.

I tried a few 2b2t copies.\
They were fun, but they felt empty.

So I joined **constantiam.net**.\
It is pure anarchy.\
No `/sethome`.\
No `/tpa`.\
No shortcuts.

You move the old way: walking, boating, riding, and surviving.

This challenge made me enjoy Minecraft again.\
I played for days without stopping.\
I now have one goal:\
**build a home millions of blocks from spawn**, hidden and safe, and slowly create a life there.

And this time, I’m not planning to quit.
